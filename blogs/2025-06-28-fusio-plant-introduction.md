<p>With this post we like to introduce our new project <a href="https://github.com/apioo/fusio-plant">Plant</a>, a server management tool
to easily self-host Fusio and other apps on your server.</p>

<p>The goal of Plant is to provide builders an easy way to self-host multiple apps on a server. It can be seen as a modern lightweight
alternative to cPanel or Plesk with a simple, performant and clean tech-stack. Since Plant is also based on Fusio it provides a complete
REST API. We have just released a first version, so feel free to check out the <a href="https://github.com/apioo/fusio-plant">GitHub repository</a>
in case you are interested in an easy and cost effective way to self-host apps. Plant is, of course, perfectly designed to run Fusio, but it is also
possible to run any other web-app that provides a docker image.</p>

<p>If you want to provide some feedback, you can jump on our <a href="https://discord.gg/eMrMgwsc6e">Discord</a> or you can also create an issue
at the <a href="https://github.com/apioo/fusio-plant/issues">Plant repository</a>.</p>
